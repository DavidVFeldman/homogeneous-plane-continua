# Inventory: what the proof of `s3_span_nine` is stated for (batch 73, §2.3)

This is the inventory the batch-69 commission asked for once `s3_span_nine` became a theorem, and
it is **a statement only**: nothing here is a claim about any span other than nine.

**How it was taken.** The kernel-level dependency graph of `HPC.Fold.s3_span_nine` was walked in
the compiled environment; 494 declarations of the `HPC.Fold` namespace occur in it. 471 of them are
declarations written in the repository and are listed below; the remaining 23 are compiler
generated (equation lemmas `_.eq_1`, match auxiliaries `_.match_1`, structure projections of
`IsCrookedIvF`, and three `Decidable` instances) and carry no statement of their own. They are:
`HighAt.eq_1`, `HighLate.eq_1`, `IsCrookedIvF.adj`, `IsCrookedIvF.crooked`, `IsCrookedIvF.le`,
`IsCrookedIvF.mk`, `IsCrookedIvF.width`, `Leg3Ceil.eq_1`, `Leg3Valley.eq_1`, `LowAt.eq_1`,
`LowLate.eq_1`, `gseq.match_1`, `gseq3.match_1`, `gseq4.match_1`, `instDecidableVal3`,
`instDecidableVal4`, `instDecidableVal7`, `lamLen.eq_1`, `minGap.match_1_1`, `pseq.match_1`,
`z.match_1`, `z_mono_aux.match_1_1`, `z_pos_aux.match_1_1`.

**The criterion, stated exactly.** A declaration is put in the first list when a literal level
offset — a textual `L + k`, `L - k`, `H + k` or `H - k` — occurs in its statement (for a
definition, in its body); otherwise in the second. So the first list is *everything whose statement
pins a level relative to the floor or the ceiling of the band*, which is the syntactic form of
"stated for nine alone", and the second list is everything whose statement carries its levels as
parameters. The criterion is syntactic, and two caveats follow from that:

* a declaration in the first list may still be true and usable at other spans — it is listed
  because its **statement** fixes offsets, not because it is known to fail elsewhere; the branch
  levels `L + 4` and `L + 5`, the band `[L, L + 9]`, the announcement levels `L + 1` and `L + 8`,
  the walk levels `L + 1`, `L + 2`, `L + 3`, `L + 6`, `L + 7`, `L + 8`, `L + 9`, and the
  hypothesis `H ≤ L + 9` of `s3_span_nine_of_mid` are all of this kind;
* a declaration in the second list may still be *used* only at nine: the timing constants come
  through `z` (`z 3 = 6`, `z 4 = 13`, `z 5 = 30`, `z 6 = 71`, `z 7 = 170`) and through `minGap`,
  whose statements are generic in the number of levels while every call site in this proof supplies
  a number of levels fixed by the band.

The walks are the fixed part in the strongest sense. The branch at `L + 5` is closed by the three
walks of `leg4Land_tri` — `Leg4Land f (L+9) (L+8) (L+1) (L+5)` (`w8`) and the two reflected ones,
`Leg4Land (armRefl …) (L+9) (L+6) (L+1) (L+4)` (`w3`) and `Leg4Land (armRefl …) (L+9) (L+6) (L+2)
(L+4)` (`wb`) — together with `w71 = Leg4Land f (L+9) (L+7) (L+1) (L+5)` and `w72 = Leg4Land f
(L+9) (L+7) (L+2) (L+5)`; the branch at `L + 4` is closed by the Λ-walk, the V-walk and the
three-leg walk `L+3 → L+6 → L+1 → L+8`. Each is a named tuple of integer levels, and the criteria
that select them (`Ceil71`, `Ceil72`, `Deep72`, `Mid2Floor`, `DeepFirst`, `DeepFirst2`, `HighRunG`,
`HighRunT`, `LowAt`, `HighAt`, `LamAnn`, `VeeAnn`) are all in the first list.
