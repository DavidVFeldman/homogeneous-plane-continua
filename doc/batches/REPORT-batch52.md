Runs on top of batch 51. Nothing in §1 of the commission is proved; one statement the commission
asks about is **refuted**, and it is reported first. All Lean below is `sorry`-free except the one
new work item named as such, and every new theorem depends only on `propext`, `Classical.choice`

## 0. The refutation: three walks are not enough at the branch `L + 5`

Question 3 asks whether the arm-only half of the branch `L + 5` is the reflection of the branch
`L + 4`'s. Half of it is (§3.3 below). The half that concerns the *list of walks* is not:

> **`not_threeWalks5Arms`** (`HPC/Fold/ReflectMiss.lean`). There are two 1-Lipschitz arms of the
> band `[0, 9]`, based at `L + 5`, both `LowAt (L + 1) (L + 8)` — the branch hypothesis — both
> satisfying `CrookUp` and `CrookDown` — the two crookedness clauses the model delivers for every
> arm of a faithful pair — with the pair served by neither reflected canonical walk, and with the
> backward arm folding onto **none** of the three reflected walks.

The two arms are the first fifty-seven steps of the two arms at a basepoint of the branch `L + 5`
of a mixture of shavings and fills of `bing 0 9`, found by `gap-batch52.c`; every verdict in the
file — `IsLip`, the band, the basepoint, `LowAt`, `CrookUp`, `CrookDown` and the seven fold
verdicts — is decided in the Lean kernel, and where the arms come from is not part of the
statement.

**The definition audit that found it.** The predicates the census evaluates at the branch `L + 5`
are the reflected clauses of `HPC/Fold/ArmReflect.lean` and the five reflected walks. They are
audited against their definitions in `HPC/Fold/Audit52.lean`, generated by `gen-audit52.py` from
the census's own sample: 14 arms of the census, of 55 to 64 steps, 13 predicates each, 182 kernel
checks, every one agreeing with the census. The census also audits the transport theorem itself —
`folds(f, reflected walk)` against `folds(9 − f, walk)` — 3 126 010 checks, 0 failures — and the
three reflected criteria, 1 875 606 checks, 0 failures.

**How large the failure is.** Over the whole corpus, 771 of the 74 884 unserved basepoints of the
branch `L + 5` are served by none of the three reflected walks. They are all in mixtures of several
shavings and fills; the thin chains, the single shavings and the single fills have none, which is
why no earlier batch could have seen this. Adding the two *unreflected* walks
`L+5 → L+7 → L+1 → L+8` and `L+4 → L+7 → L+1 → L+8` — the shortest walks the census finds serving
the missed pairs — still leaves 166 of them unserved. So the branch `L + 5` is not a list of three
walks, and not a list of five either.

## 2. The questions

### 2.1 The ceiling case as a crossing

Asked in `HPC/Fold/CrossCeil.lean`, and the file is the answer as far as this batch takes it.

* **Proved.** An arm failing `Leg3Ceil` does not fold onto `w₃` (`not_foldsOnto_w3_of_not_leg3Ceil`,
  from the necessity half of the criterion), so in the ceiling case the first of the three walks is
  out and only `w₄` and `w₅` remain — which is why the question is asked of those two.
* **Proved.** If one arm fails `Leg3Ceil` and the pair does not fold onto the Λ-walk, the other arm
  satisfies `Leg3Ceil` (`leg3Ceil_other_of_not_lam`): the failing arm folds onto the Λ-walk by
  `leg3Ceil_or_lam`, so a second failing arm would have the pair served by that walk.
* **The work item.** `cross_ceil_sentence`: in that configuration both arms satisfy the sentence of
  `w₄`, or both satisfy the sentence of `w₅`. With `ceil_case_three_walks_of_sentence` — proved —
  this closes the ceiling case of `unserved_three_walks`.
* **The census.** Of 256 183 branch basepoints, 12 373 have an arm failing `Leg3Ceil`; 521 of those
  also miss the Λ-walk, which is the configuration the question is about, and **at every one of the
  521 the answer is yes**. The second hypothesis is not decoration: without it the implication is
  false — 168 basepoints have an arm failing `Leg3Ceil` and satisfy neither sentence, and every one
  of them is served by the Λ-walk, so the pair is served but not by `w₄` or `w₅`.

### 2.2 `ValleyResidual` as a pair

**Negative, over this corpus: the configuration is not realised.** `census-batch52.c` counts, at
every branch basepoint, the pairs in which one arm fails `Leg3Valley` *and* an arm realises the
residual configuration of `cross_valley_profile` (all its low times before its first ascent to
`L + 7` at its step two or three). Over 1 369 faithful pairs and 49 465 basepoints with a failing
valley arm there are **0** such pairs, and at every one of the 49 465 both arms fold onto the V-walk
— `cross_valley_profile` itself holds throughout. So the item is not refutable over the generators
of this repository, and the residual case of batch 50 is, on this evidence, vacuous for pairs: an
arm realising it never sits opposite an arm that fails the clause. That is not a proof that it
cannot; excluding it remains open.

### 2.3 The branch `L + 5` as the reflection of the branch `L + 4`

The answer is in three parts: the criteria reflect, the crookedness clauses do not, the list of
walks does not.

**(a) The criteria reflect — proved.** `HPC/Fold/ArmReflect.lean` defines the reflection of an arm,
`armRefl a f = fun j => a − f j`, and transports along it: `armRefl_isLip`, `foldsOnto_armRefl`
(a fold onto a reflected walk is a fold of the reflected arm), the walk identities `w3R_eq`,
`w4R_eq`, `w5R_eq`, and — through a translation lemma for the Λ-itinerary — `veeCenter_armRefl`,
`lamCenter_armRefl`, `veeLen_armRefl`, `lamLen_armRefl`, so that the reflection of the Λ-walk based
at `p` is the V-walk based at `L + H − p`. Each landmark clause has a reflection, obtained by
reading `L + k` as `L + 9 − k` (`Ceil4R`, `Floor3R`, `MidW4R`, `FloorW4R`, `TopW4R`, `Ceil5R`,
`BottomW5R`), and each of the three criteria of batch 51 has its exact mirror at the basepoint
`L + 5`: `foldsOnto_w3R_iff_landmark`, `foldsOnto_w4R_iff_landmark`, `foldsOnto_w5R_iff_landmark`.
From them `three_walks5_iff_sentence` and `s3_span_nine_mid5_of_sentence`. No symmetry of the model
is used anywhere: reflecting one arm is a relabelling of the levels of one 1-Lipschitz sequence.

**(b) The crookedness clauses do not — refuted.** `CrookUp` and `CrookDown` are not reflections of
one another. The reflection of `CrookDown` is `CrookUpR`, whose two windows sit one level above
those of `CrookUp` (`crookUpR_iff_crookDown_armRefl`), and the model delivers `CrookUp`. So the
reflected dichotomies hold only with the reflected clauses as hypotheses (`ceil4R_or_lam`,
`floor3R_or_vee`, `three_walks5_or_cross`), and those hypotheses are not available:
`HPC/Fold/ArmReflectGap.lean` exhibits a sixteen-step arm of the band based at `L + 5` satisfying
`CrookUp` and `CrookDown` and failing `CrookUpR` (`crookUpR_not_of_model`), and a five-step one for
`CrookDownR` (`crookDownR_not_of_model`); both were found by exhaustive search
(`search-batch52.c`) and both are decided in the kernel. The model agrees: over the census corpus
`CrookUpR` fails on 12 615 and `CrookDownR` on 10 931 of the 105 203 arms of the branch `L + 5` on
which they were tested, while `CrookUp` and `CrookDown` — theorems — fail on none. And the
dichotomy is not merely unprovable but false in the model: the census finds 93 arms failing
`Floor3R` that do not fold onto the V-walk based at `L + 6`; `gap-batch52.c` extracts the shortest
such arm it can reach, of 175 steps.

**(c) The list of walks does not** — that is §0.

So the two branches do **not** share everything but the crossings. They share the engine and the
criteria; the branch `L + 5` needs its own walks, and its own reading of crookedness.

## 3. The censuses

`census-batch52.c`, carrying over the generators, the faithfulness test (`IsCrookedIvF`) and the
fold program of `census-batch51.c` (the dynamic program proved equivalent to `FoldsOnto` in
`HPC/Fold/Decide.lean`). Generators, over the two base chains `bing 0 9` and `bing 9 0`: the thin
chains; all single shavings; all single fills; random mixtures at rate 1/3; heavy mixtures at rate
1/2 and the total mixture; random faithful perturbation walks (single-link changes of `ρ` or `σ`
kept when the pair stays faithful). Run `census-batch52 40 52 200 192`, log `census-batch52.log`.

* 1 369 faithful pairs, 1 349 834 links, of which **41 391 non-thin (3.07 %)**; by generator the
  fraction runs from 0 (the thin chains) through 0.15 % (single shavings and fills) to 38 % (heavy
  mixtures).
* Branch `L + 4`: 256 183 basepoints, 73 262 unserved by the two canonical walks; `w₃` serves
  73 194, `w₄` 10 666, `w₅` 71 453; **none is missed by all three**. `three_walks_iff_sentence`
  agrees with the folds at all of them (0 equivalence failures, 1 537 098 exactness checks).
* Branch `L + 5`: 312 601 basepoints, 74 884 unserved; the three reflected walks miss 771 (§0).
* Question 1: 12 373 ceiling-case basepoints, 521 of them Λ-free, 0 failures (§2.1).
* Question 2: 0 realisations of the residual configuration as a pair (§2.2).
* Crookedness and its reflections on 105 203 arms (§2.3 (b)).

Supporting programs: `search-batch52.c` (exhaustive depth-first search over the arms satisfying
`CrookUp` and `CrookDown`, for the shortest witnesses of the gap; a randomised mode for the longer
goals, which found none up to forty-four steps) and `gap-batch52.c` (shortest witnesses drawn from
the model, by truncating failing arms to the shortest prefix that still fails).
